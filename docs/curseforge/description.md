FishDex turns fishing into a collection. Every fish, trophy, wreck and item you can fish on WoW Forever has its place in one window, dark until you catch it. With a UI that looks like the game's own.

**Features**

- **Your fishing collection**: fish, quest fish, trophies, wrecks and chests, items, each in its own tab. A caught entry shows where to fish it and the cooking or alchemy recipes that use it (known or not by your character). One you have not caught yet shows only where to find it.
- **Only real fishing counts**: an item bought at the auction house or looted from a mob never fills the collection. Catches are shared by all your characters.
- **First catch announce** in the middle of the screen, with a sound.
- **Shiny catches**: every fish can come out shiny (rare 2 %, epic 0.5 %, legendary 0.05 %), with its own announce, colour, sound and tab. Purely cosmetic.
- **Guild ranking**: total fished and shinies of your guild mates who use FishDex. Sent in one small message after you stop fishing.
- **Cast and reel in without aiming**: double-click the world to cast; when a fish bites, double-click again and the bobber is reeled in, no need to click on it (two options, each can be turned off). Your Interact Key does the same (cast, reel in, loot), or pick a key of your own in the options. An icon shows on the bobber when it is in reach.
- **Fishing comfort**: a lure button while a fishing pole is equipped (click to apply, hover to choose), auto loot of fishing loot, friendly NPC names hidden and louder sounds while fishing. Your game settings come back 10 s after your last cast.
- **Sounds of your choice** for each announce: game sounds, or sounds shared by your other addons.

**Usage**

1. Click the minimap button (or type `/fishdex`) to open the collection.
2. Equip a fishing pole, double-click the world (or press your Interact Key) to cast, and do it again when a fish bites: new catches fill the collection and are announced.
3. "Options..." at the bottom right turns each option on or off and picks the sounds.

**Slash commands**

`/fishdex` (or `/peche`) opens the window. `/fishdex sound`, `announce`, `lure`, `minimap` toggle them; `/fishdex click` cycles the double-click (right, left, off); `/fishdex fr`, `en`, `auto` set the addon's language.

**Compatibility**

WoW Forever (Interface 16001), tested on build 1.60.1.70790. English and French.

**Good to know**

- Only catches made after installing FishDex count.
- The guild ranking needs guild mates who also use FishDex.
- The fishing zones come from Classic data: Forever may place some fish elsewhere. A catch always counts, wherever it happens.
- Shinies are rolled by the addon on your computer: they are for fun, not proof.
- If you used Better Fishing, turn it off: two double-click casters get in each other's way. Same for another addon's fishing key on the same key.
- Never while mounted or in combat, and never a second cast while the bobber is out. A bobber too far to reel in: right-click it.
