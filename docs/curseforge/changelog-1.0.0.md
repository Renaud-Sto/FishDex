**1.0.0** (first release, WoW Forever 1.60.1.70790)

New
- Collection window of everything you can fish, filled only by real fishing loot, saved per account.
- First catch announce with a sound; shiny catches (rare, epic, legendary) with their own tab.
- Guild ranking tab for guild mates who use FishDex.
- Lure button, double-click to cast, auto loot, hidden NPC names and louder sounds while fishing.
- Options window with a Sounds tab; minimap button; English and French.

Known limitations
- Fishing zones come from Classic data; a catch counts wherever it happens.
