**1.1.1** (WoW Forever 1.60.1)

Changed
- The double click has two options: "Double-click to cast" and "Double-click to reel in the bobber". Turn the second off and a click on the bobber is the game's own again.

Fixed
- A single right click on the bobber could cast again instead of reeling in: left and right click can never become a fishing key now.

Added
- `/fishdex debug` prints the bindings FishDex sets while fishing, to report a problem.

Full changelog: https://github.com/Renaud-Sto/FishDex/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md
