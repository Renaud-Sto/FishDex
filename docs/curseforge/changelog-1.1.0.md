**1.1.0** (WoW Forever 1.60.1)

New
- Cast and reel in without aiming: the same double click casts, then reels the bobber in once it is in reach. An icon shows on the bobber when it is.
- Your Interact Key fishes too: with a fishing pole, it casts, reels in and takes the loot. Near an NPC or an object it keeps its own job.
- Optional fishing key of your own, chosen in the Options window (also listed in the game's Key Bindings under FishDex).
- Options: a "Cast and reel in" section that explains how it works, and a tooltip on every fishing option.

Removed
- "Also while mounted" and "Recast while the bobber is out": FishDex never casts while mounted or while the bobber is out (a second cast loses the fish).

Full changelog: https://github.com/Renaud-Sto/FishDex/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md
